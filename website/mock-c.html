How to read this, and why it runs past midnight

Read a row across for one gate's whole day — back-to-back bars mean a hard-worked gate, gaps mean idle time you pay for. Read a column down to see how much of the airport is busy at once; the tallest column is the day's peak. The chart runs into the next morning because an aircraft that lands at 11pm and leaves at 6am is one continuous visit.
